      Welcome to the valley

      4 steps to hop glory

      1

      Pick a tool

      Right sidebar. Hop Yard to plant, Dug Well for water, Hop Kiln to dry crops, Pickers' Cabin for harvest labor.

      2

      Click the map

      Green tiles = open dirt. Blue = creek (free water). Gray = rock, dark = forest — clear those first. Your HQ is center.

      3

      Push the big button

      Advances time: Spring → Summer → Fall → Winter. Each season does something different. Harvests are automatic in Fall.

      4

      Stay out of the red

      Keep cash above -$400 or the bank takes the farm. Hit $32,000 net worth to become Hop King. Press Space to advance fast.
