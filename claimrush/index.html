How to play

Puyallup valley land grab. Three steps:

  1. 1

    Tap a square

    Open land shows a number (acres). Tap it — it’s yours.

  2. 2

    Get to 180 acres

    Hops = more acres. Blue river = can’t take. First to 180 wins.

  3. 3

    Beat the other player

    Practice vs the computer, or 1v1 a friend with a short code.
